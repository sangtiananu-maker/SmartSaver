# Privacy Policy — SmartSaver

**Last updated: September 18, 2026**
**Developer: Tian**

---

## Overview

SmartSaver ("the App") is a free price comparison and discount calculator application available as a Progressive Web App (PWA) and Android application. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information.

---

## 1. Information We Do NOT Collect

SmartSaver is designed with privacy first. The App does **not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, including:

- Name, email address, or contact information
- Account credentials or login information
- Location data (GPS)
- Camera or microphone access
- Device identifiers or advertising IDs
- Browsing history or usage analytics
- Financial or payment information

---

## 2. How the App Works

SmartSaver operates **entirely on your device (local only)**:

- All calculations (price comparison, pro-rata discount) are performed **locally in your browser or on your device**.
- No data is sent to any server, database, or third party.
- The App works **100% offline** using a Service Worker — no internet connection is required after initial load.
- Language preference (Thai/English) and theme (Dark/Light) are stored locally in your browser's `localStorage` and never leave your device.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services

SmartSaver does **not** integrate with any third-party services, including:

- Google Analytics or Firebase Analytics
- Google Ads or any advertising networks
- Facebook SDK or social login
- Crash reporting tools
- Payment processors

The App is hosted on **GitHub Pages**, a static hosting service by GitHub, Inc. GitHub may collect standard server access logs (IP address, browser type, referring URL) as part of their platform infrastructure. Please refer to [GitHub's Privacy Policy](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) for details.

---

## 4. Data Storage

The only data stored is:

| Data | Where Stored | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Language preference (Thai/EN) | Browser `localStorage` | Remember your language choice |
| Theme preference (Dark/Light) | Browser `localStorage` | Remember your display preference |
| Cached app files | Browser Cache (Service Worker) | Enable offline use |

This data is stored solely on your device and can be cleared at any time by clearing your browser's storage or uninstalling the app.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy

SmartSaver does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). Since the App collects no personal information from anyone, it is safe for all ages.

---

## 6. Security

Because SmartSaver does not transmit any data over the internet, there is no risk of data interception or server-side breach from the App itself.
